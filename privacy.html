Privacy Policy

Chord Halo is developed by John Lazarus. The app works on your iPhone without an account, and the developer collects no data from it.

The microphone

Chord Halo listens to the music around you to find its chords, key and tempo. This happens on your iPhone, and the audio is never sent anywhere. Audio is recorded only when you teach Chord Halo a song by playing it, and that recording is kept with the song on your iPhone until you delete it.

Information stored on your iPhone

Your songs, their chord sheets, recordings you make while learning a song, audio files you import, and your settings are stored in the app on your iPhone. The app does not operate a server, account or analytics service, and does not provide its own cloud sync. Apple’s device backups and system services may handle app data according to your settings and Apple’s privacy practices.

Record a take uses Apple’s screen recording. The recording is shown in Apple’s preview, where you choose to save or share it; Chord Halo does not keep or upload it.

Purchases

Chord Halo Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app checks the purchase with Apple’s StoreKit on your device. The developer receives no payment details and no personal information from the app.

How information is shared

Nothing is collected by the developer, so nothing is sold, rented or shared with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties. The app contains no advertising and no tracking.

Retention and deletion

Songs and recordings stay on your iPhone until you delete them in the app or delete the app. See Data deletion for details.

If you contact support

Emailing support shares your email address, message and any attachments with the developer and the email provider used to handle the conversation. This information is used to respond to your request, not for marketing or tracking. Contact us if you would like the correspondence deleted, subject to any legally required retention.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics scripts, tracking cookies or embedded third-party content. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Chord Halo collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, email ronnie.lazarus.dev@gmail.com.
