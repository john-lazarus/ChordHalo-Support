Help when you need it

Support

Chord Halo shows the chords, key and tempo of the music around you, and lets you play along. Here’s how to get the most from it.

Requirements

An iPhone running iOS 27 or later. Chord Halo needs access to the microphone to listen.

Get started

  1. Open Chord Halo and allow microphone access when asked.
  2. Play a song nearby, from a speaker, an instrument or another device.
  3. The chord appears as it plays. The key, tempo and capo appear once Chord Halo is confident.

Chord Halo isn’t hearing anything

  1. Check that microphone access is on: Settings → Apps → Chord Halo → Microphone.
  2. Make sure another app isn’t using the microphone, such as a call or a recording.
  3. Move closer to the music, or turn it up a little.

The tempo hasn’t appeared

Chord Halo shows a tempo only once it is confident, and then keeps it for the song. Songs without a steady beat may take longer or show no tempo. You can set it yourself with Tap, or halve or double it.

Chord Halo Pro

Pro is a single in-app purchase that adds every sound, unlimited songs, Import, seventh chords, MIDI out, and piano and ukulele shapes. It is shared with your family through Family Sharing. If Pro is missing on a new device, open Settings in Chord Halo and choose Restore Purchases. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.
