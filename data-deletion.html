Data deletion

Chord Halo has no account and no server. Your songs, recordings and settings are stored only on your iPhone.

Delete a song

In Songs, swipe left on a song and tap Delete. This removes the song, its chord sheet and any recording made while learning it.

Remove all app data

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Chord Halo.
  2. Choose Delete App.

Use Delete App, not Offload App: offloading keeps the app’s documents and data. A Pro purchase is kept by Apple and can be restored after reinstalling.

Device backups

Deleting the app does not remove older device backups. Apple’s iCloud or computer backups may retain app information and restore it later. Manage those copies through Apple’s backup settings. The developer cannot access or delete your device backups.
